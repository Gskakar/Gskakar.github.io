## Problem

Reports were assembled by hand every cycle, and figures didn't always match across systems.

## What I did

- **Defined KPIs** for learner performance and retention with management.
- **Validated MySQL data** against SharePoint records before it reached any report.
- **Built a Power BI model with DAX** on demographics, course completion, geographic reach, and trends.

## Result

Automated reporting, and insights that supported a **~20% increase in user interaction**.
