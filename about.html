About

I grew up in Delhi and moved to Edmonton in 2022 to study Computing Science at the University of Alberta. Courses in statistics, data analysis, and databases got me interested in how data supports decisions, and internships turned that into a job.

These days I rebuild university ranking methodologies for the Alberta School of Business and analyze household survey data on energy access. I care about getting the numbers right: I check figures against their source records before they go into a report.

Outside of work I play in an amateur football league and follow European football closely, especially the tactics. I also sing and perform, read biographies, and cook when life gets busy.

I also spent a year as Vice President Internal of the International Students' Association, and received the UASU Global Connection Award for that work.
